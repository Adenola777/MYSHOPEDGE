THE 15 SCREENS, on 8 sheets
  Files 02 to 09. Each sheet carries two screens side by side except the last,
  which carries S15 alone. The S number is printed on each screen in the image.
  Only files 02 and 09 have their screen titles in the filename, because those
  are the two I opened and read. Files 03 to 08 are numbered by their position
  in the document and their titles are on the images themselves.

THE 5 WORKFLOWS
  01  End-to-end platform workflow
  10  Onboarding workflow
  11  Product Costs upload and matching workflow
  12  Return lifecycle workflow
  13  Discrepancy detection and resolution workflow
  14  Backend orchestration, from trigger to notification

  That is six diagrams, and the document says "one end-to-end workflow and four
  detailed workflows". The sixth, file 14, is backend orchestration, which is
  arguably not a user workflow. The count in the document's own summary is one
  short of what the document contains.

WHAT THIS IS NOT
  These are low-fidelity wireframes. The document says so itself: they "show
  structure, content and behaviour, not final visual design".
  They cover S1 to S15 only. S16 to S38 were ruled later, in A3, A14 and A18,
  and were never wireframed.
  At least one is out of date against a later ruling: S2 shows "Orders, last 12
  months", and history is now twenty four months on every plan.
